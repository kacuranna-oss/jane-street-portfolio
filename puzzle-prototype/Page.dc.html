Rules at a glance
  1. Cover the grid with symmetric states. No overlaps.
  2. A capitol ★ is the one cell that never moves. Some states have none.
  3. A step costs the state's size. At a border, pay for the smaller state.
  4. A number is the cheapest trip to a capitol.
  5. Write state sizes in the cells. Answer = sum of each (row total)².
